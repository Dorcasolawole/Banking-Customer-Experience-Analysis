# Banking Customer Experience & Complaint Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyses 51,065 banking-related consumer complaints submitted between 2017 and 2023.

The goal is to understand complaint patterns, identify common banking issues, examine company response timeliness, and evaluate complaint resolution outcomes using Power BI.

## Project Objectives

- Analyse complaints across different banking products.
- Identify the most common consumer complaint issues.
- Examine monthly complaint trends.
- Understand the channels through which consumers submit complaints.
- Evaluate company response timeliness and resolution outcomes.
- Compare complaint-handling performance across banking products.


### 1. Banking Customer Experience Overview

This dashboard presents:
- Total complaints and resolution KPIs.
- Complaints by banking product.
- Top five complaint issues.
- Monthly complaint trends.
- Complaints by submission channel.
- Top ten states by complaint volume.

### 2. Banking Service Performance

This dashboard explores:
- Timely and late company responses.
- Monetary and non-monetary relief cases.
- Company response categories.
- Complaint resolution status.
- Resolution status by product.
- Response timeliness by product.

  ![Banking Service Performance](Banking%20Service%20Performance.jpg)
## Key Findings

- 51,065 banking-related complaints were analysed.
- Checking and savings accounts recorded the highest complaint volume.
- The closure rate was 97.58% among complaints with recorded resolution status.
- 94.23% of all complaints were recorded as receiving timely responses.
